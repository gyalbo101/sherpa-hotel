Why trekkers stop here

A gentler, faster way up to Gosainkunda

Most pilgrims climb the steep trail from Dhunche to Chandanbari (Sing Gompa). From Thulo Bharkhu the trail is gentler and faster, with wide views back over Dhunche and the surrounding mountains.

Arrive from Kathmandu, sleep well, eat a Sherpa dinner, and start fresh in the morning with breakfast included.

Trails from our door

Walk straight out into Langtang National Park

🏔️

Gosainkunda

Via Chandanbari (Sing Gompa) and Laurebina to the sacred lakes, a Ramsar site at about 4,380 m.

🥾

Brabal & Thulo Syabru

Through pine forest to Brabal, then on to Thulo Syabru, where you join the main Langtang Valley trail.

🧭

Langtang Valley

With Syabrubesi damaged by the 2026 flood, many Langtang treks now start from the Dhunche / Thulo Bharkhu side.

Foreign trekkers in Langtang National Park need a licensed guide, a park entry permit and a TIMS card. Ask your agency, or call us and we'll point you to local guides.

Getting here

Kathmandu → Dhunche → Thulo Bharkhu

  1. Kathmandu to Dhunche
    By bus or jeep via Trishuli. Dhunche is the Rasuwa district headquarters and the Langtang National Park entry point.
  2. Dhunche to Thulo Bharkhu
    A short drive on along the Pasang Lhamu Highway. We're right on the road.
  3. Start walking
    Next morning, head up to Chandanbari (Sing Gompa) for Gosainkunda, or to Brabal for Thulo Syabru and Langtang.
Road conditions after the 2026 flood Roads in Rasuwa are still being repaired and conditions change quickly. Call us before you travel and we'll tell you today's situation.
